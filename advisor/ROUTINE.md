# Manuel's CEO routine

*Least effort, most impact. Three moves a week. The rest is maintenance, done fast or delegated.*

## What it borrows, and why
I kept only what fits a ~50-person creative company run by four partners:

- **Drucker, *The Effective Executive*.** Know where your time actually goes. Ask "what can only I contribute?" Use big blocks, not fragments.
- **Porter & Nohria, "How CEOs Manage Time" (HBR, 2018).** CEOs who protect time alone, and spend about 25% of it on relationships outside the building, outperform. Without design, email and reactive meetings eat everything.
- **EOS / Traction.** A *Visionary* (you) plus an *Integrator* (Ju, by profile) running a weekly meeting on a fixed agenda, with 3 quarterly "rocks". It's the simplest operating system at this size.
- **Grove, *High Output Management*.** Your output is the output of your units. Your best lever is the people you lead: 1:1s, and decisions made early.
- **Bezos, one-way and two-way doors.** Reversible decisions get made fast or delegated. Only irreversible ones get your deep time.
- **McKinsey, "CEO Excellence".** Six jobs: direction, alignment, mobilizing leaders, the board, external stakeholders, personal effectiveness. Your five areas map onto them.

## The week

| When | Block | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon 07:30–08:15** | **Choose the 3** | Read the advisor's brief (5 min). Pick the 3 moves. Write them in #mtt-board in one line each. |
| **Tue (board meeting)** | **The four** | A fixed agenda: decisions first, then numbers, then the 3 moves. The advisor prepares the pre-read the night before. |
| **Tue 09:00** | **Signals** | #DIGITAL-LIFE status (Alexia + Michelle) is already automated. Just read it. |
| **Wed + Fri 07:00–09:00** | **Deep work** | The long arc: offers, the client map, 2027, writing. No meetings, no Slack. |
| **Thu** | **Outside** | Clients and prospects, one real conversation outside the building. The 25% rule. |
| **Thu 16:00** | **Read growth-signals** | The BUs post on Wed/Thu. The advisor turns them into stuck points (15 min). |
| **Fri 17:00** | **Close the week** | Did the 3 moves happen? One line each. The advisor drafts the board note. |
| Daily, 2 × 20 min | **Maintenance** | Slack and email in two windows (late morning and end of day). Outside them, you're not on call. |

The 5am writing, boxing and time with Vicente go on the calendar first, before anything else. The advisor flags any week where they get eaten.

## The rule for the 3 moves
A move qualifies only if all three are true:
1. **Only you can do it.** It's a decision, a relationship, or a direction.
2. **It unblocks someone else's work, or the number.**
3. **It takes 2 hours or less of your time.** If it's bigger, it isn't a move. It's a project, and it needs an owner.

Everything else goes into one of three bins: **Delegate** (an owner and a date, through the duo), **Decide in 2 minutes** (a reversible door), or **Drop** (say no).

## Quarterly
- **3 rocks for the company**, set with the four. The 2027 plan is the obvious Q1 rock.
- **One review of the strategic map** (`MAP.md`): client concentration, offer coverage, the dream-30 list.
- **One personal check-in**: energy, health, where the role is costing you more than it should.
