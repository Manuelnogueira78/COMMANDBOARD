Growing is continuing
(Culture post, MATTER OS internal console)

We grow close to people.

Close to people, we listen before we speak. And listening first, we notice what they need before they ask. That’s what people remember, and what they remember decides who they come back to.

This week was like that. Luisa went to an in-person workshop in New York, and it turned into a new brief. Kika’s team sat down with Waze before the first presentation, to open the next projects.

Being called back is earned in the finish. The tight deadline met, on time. On time, and beautiful. The way we dress when we want to be seen well.

Seen well, we go further. Another country, another rule, another culture. This week a local rule in Mexico blocked an idea, and the Waze team adapted in days. They could, because they were prepared.

The next frontier is the one between us, and it’s the easiest to cross. We have very good art directors, writers and strategists solving similar problems for different clients, and we have each other’s numbers. Before your work goes to the client, send it to a colleague outside your project. Ask how they would solve it. Let’s talk more between peers.

I think a lot about phases.

I miss my son as a baby. Small, learning to laugh, asleep on me with his little arm on my shoulder, needing me for everything. I miss him at 3. At 4. At 8. I’ll miss him at 10 too. And I can’t imagine who he’ll be at 18. So many incredible things are still coming, and each one will be worth missing.

Growing means change. Good news, and things we leave behind. Pains that passed, jokes we’ve outgrown. A full night’s sleep, finally. Until the next good reason to stay awake.

I’ve been looking after a part of MATTER called GROWTH. Knowing me, you know that means time. Growing is continuing: caring for what we nurtured while it changes, standing strong in small increments.

Anyone who has built a sandcastle learns this early. The ones that last have a base. So you learn the base, the process, and the structure grows with the potential. The stronger the base, the higher you can build.

MATTER will go through phases. So will I. So will each of us. Growing is adding layers, and making room for new shapes. And seeing how beautiful what already exists is. It could simply not exist, and here it is.

Knowing how to see.
Seeing, knowing how to care.
Caring, and continuing.
