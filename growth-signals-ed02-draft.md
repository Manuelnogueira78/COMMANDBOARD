We grow close to the client.
Close to the client, we listen before we present.
Listening first, we come back with the answer they haven't asked for yet.
The answer they didn't ask for is the one they remember.
And what they remember decides who they call again.

This week was like that. An in-person workshop turned into a new brief. A conversation before a presentation was set up to open the next projects.

Being called again is trust.
Trust is earned in the finish.
In the finish: the tight deadline met, on time.
On time, and beautiful.
Beautiful the way we dress when we want to be seen well.

The better we dress, the better we are perceived. That holds for people, and it holds for work.

Seen well, we go further.
Further is another country, another rule, another culture.
This week a local rule blocked an idea, and the team adapted in days.
In days, because they were prepared.
Prepared, no frontier is a wall.

The next frontier is the one between us, and it is the easiest to cross.
We have very good art directors, writers and strategists solving similar problems for different clients.
We have each other's numbers.
Send your work to a colleague outside your project before it goes to the client. Ask how they would solve it. Let's talk more between peers.

I think a lot about phases.

I miss my son as a baby. Small, learning to laugh. Carried in my arms with his little arm on my shoulder, then asleep on me. Needing me for everything.
I miss him at 3. At 4. At 8.
I will miss him at 10 too.
And I can't imagine who he will be at 18. So many incredible things are still coming, and each one will be worth missing.

That is what living is.

Growing means change. Good news, and things we leave behind. Pains that passed, and jokes we've outgrown. A full night's sleep, finally. Until the next good reason to stay awake.

Nobody cries when a caterpillar becomes a butterfly. The two are one and the same, in different phases. Maybe the caterpillar was even more beautiful. Both are worth seeing.

I have been talking a lot about, and taking care of, a part of MATTER called GROWTH. As you know me, that means understanding how much growth has to do with time.
Growing is continuing.
Continuing, caring for what we nurtured.
Caring for what we have, while it changes.
Changing, and standing strong over time, in small increments.

Anyone who has built a sandcastle learns this early. The castles that last are the ones with a base. So you learn the fundamentals. The base. The process. Structure and potential grow together: the stronger the base, the higher you can build. You calculate it carefully, and then you build.

MATTER will go through phases. So will I. So will each of us. Growing is adding layers, density, and making room for new shapes.

Growing is continuing to be, a little more each time.

And seeing how beautiful what already exists is. Because all of it is. It could simply not exist, and here it is.
Knowing how to see.
Seeing, knowing how to care.
Caring, and continuing.
Continuing, growing.
